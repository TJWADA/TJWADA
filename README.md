## Featured Projects

### [Mixture of Finfluencers (MoFF)](https://github.com/TJWADA/MoFF)
Web app that backtests historical predictions from YouTube finance influencers to quantify their credibility. Standardizes unstructured trading advice from video transcripts with OpenAI, scores cleaned predictions against market baselines via Alpaca, and presents credibility profiles for each influencer.

`Next.js` · `TypeScript` · `OpenAI` · `Alpaca`

### [BetterReminders](https://github.com/TJWADA/BetterReminders)
iOS reminder app that captures spoken tasks from the iPhone Action Button, transcribes them on-device with Apple Speech, and stores them locally in SwiftData. OpenAI extracts metadata — descriptions, due dates, and priority — and categorizes reminders into user-created lists.

`Swift` · `SwiftUI` · `SwiftData` · `Speech` · `OpenAI`
